Home Care Tips & Emergency Guide

🚨

Emergency First Steps — Before the Technician Arrives

A water leak, power outage, or gas smell at home can be alarming. Knowing what to do in the first few minutes can prevent serious damage. Follow these universal steps first, then scroll to the relevant section below for detailed guidance.

1 Stay calm — don't panic
2 Identify the type of emergency
3 Shut off the source (water / electricity)
4 Keep family away from danger zone
5 Document damage with photos
⚡

Electrical

Power outages, tripped breakers, wiring, and electrical safety

🔥
Burning Smell or Sparking Outlet
This is a fire hazard — act immediately:
  • Turn off the main breaker in your electrical panel right away.
  • Do NOT use the outlet or switch until inspected by a professional.
  • If you see flames, leave the house and call the fire department.
  • Never pour water on an electrical fire — use a dry powder extinguisher.
🚨 Emergency
🔌
Electrical Safety at Home
Habits that prevent dangerous situations:
  • Never overload outlets with multi-socket adapters — especially with high-wattage appliances.
  • Replace frayed or cracked cords immediately.
  • Use childproof outlet covers if you have young children.
  • Keep all electrical panels accessible and labeled clearly.
  • Install smoke detectors — test them monthly.
⚠️ Safety
💡
Before the Electrician Arrives
Help your electrician diagnose the issue faster:
  • Write down what happened: what you were using, when it stopped, any sounds or smells.
  • Note which specific rooms or outlets are affected.
  • Turn off the main breaker if any hazard is suspected.
  • Clear access to the electrical panel and affected areas.
💡 Helpful

Plumbing

Leaks, blocked drains, water pressure issues, and emergency shutoffs

💧
Water Leak Emergency — What to Do First
When a pipe bursts or you notice a leak, act immediately:
  • Find your main shutoff valve — usually near the water meter or under the kitchen sink — and turn it off immediately.
  • Open all cold-water taps to drain remaining water from pipes.
  • Place towels or buckets to limit water damage to flooring and furniture.
  • Turn off electrical switches in the affected room — water and electricity are lethal together.
  • Take photos of the damage before calling a plumber.
🚨 Emergency
🚿
Low Water Pressure — Common Causes
Before calling a plumber, check these yourself:
  • Clean faucet aerators — unscrew and soak in vinegar overnight.
  • Check the main shutoff valve is fully open.
  • Look for visible pipe kinks under sinks.
  • If multiple outlets are affected, the issue may be with your building supply — contact your landlord or municipality first.
💡 Maintenance
🔧
Routine Plumbing Maintenance
Monthly and annual habits to avoid major problems:
  • Check under all sinks for drips every month.
  • Flush water heater sediment annually.
  • Never pour cooking grease or coffee grounds down drains.
  • Know where your main shutoff valve is before an emergency.
🗓️ Preventive

Carpentry

Wood care, door fixes, furniture repairs, and kitchen cabinets

🪵
Protecting Wood Furniture
Extend the life of your wooden pieces:
  • Keep wood away from direct sunlight to prevent fading and cracking.
  • Use coasters for drinks — moisture rings are hard to remove.
  • Polish with a damp cloth (not wet) and dry immediately.
  • Apply beeswax or furniture oil every 6 months for solid wood.
  • Fill small scratches with a matching wood touch-up marker.
🛡️ Preventive
🍳
Kitchen Cabinet Maintenance
Kitchens take the most abuse in any home:
  • Tighten loose hinges with a screwdriver before they cause door misalignment.
  • Clean cabinet interiors quarterly — moisture and grease cause swelling.
  • Avoid hanging wet dish towels on cabinet doors — they warp the wood over time.
  • If a hinge is broken, replace it quickly — a misaligned door can damage the whole frame.
🗓️ Maintenance
🔨
When to Call a Carpenter
Some jobs are always best left to professionals:
  • Structural damage to door or window frames.
  • Warped or swollen kitchen units that won't close properly.
  • Custom built-in shelving, closets, or partitions.
  • Stairs with loose steps or broken banisters — this is a safety issue.
👷 Professional

Blacksmithing & Metal Work

Iron doors, gates, railings, window grilles, and fence maintenance

🦀
Dealing with Rust on Metal
Early rust treatment prevents expensive replacements:
  • For surface rust, scrub with steel wool or a wire brush.
  • Apply a rust converter spray — it chemically transforms rust into a protective layer.
  • Once dry, apply anti-rust paint to seal the surface.
  • Inspect exterior iron gates and railings every spring, especially in humid climates.
🛡️ Preventive
🚪
Iron Door and Gate Care
Keep your iron doors operating smoothly and looking sharp:
  • Lubricate hinges with grease or WD-40 every 6 months.
  • Check gate locking mechanisms annually — misalignment causes lock failures.
  • Repaint iron surfaces every 2–3 years to prevent corrosion.
  • Check that welds and joints are solid — cracked welds near children's spaces are dangerous.
🗓️ Maintenance
🏗️
Window Grilles & Railings
Safety checks you should do yourself:
  • Push and shake stair railings — any movement means loose anchor bolts.
  • Check that window grille bars are still parallel and not bent — bent bars can create gaps unsafe for children.
  • Look for paint bubbling, which indicates rust forming underneath.
  • Never DIY weld or cut metal railing — call a blacksmith for structural repairs.
⚠️ Safety

Moving Companies

Smart packing, furniture protection, and stress-free relocation

📦
Packing Smart — Before Moving Day
Preparation is everything:
  • Start packing 2–3 weeks early — pack one room at a time.
  • Label every box with the room it belongs to AND a short contents list.
  • Use clothing and towels to wrap fragile items instead of wasting bubble wrap.
  • Pack a "First Night Box" — essentials you'll need immediately at the new place.
  • Photograph the back of electronics before disconnecting cables.
📋 Planning
🛋️
Protecting Large Furniture
Prevent scratches, dents, and fabric damage during the move:
  • Wrap sofas and mattresses in plastic sheeting or protective covers.
  • Disassemble beds, shelves, and tables when possible — keep all screws in labeled bags.
  • Use moving blankets on corners and glass surfaces.
  • Measure doorways before moving day — knowing what fits where saves hours.
🛡️ Protective
✅
On Moving Day Checklist
Don't leave until you've done this:
  • Check every room, cabinet, and closet — including roof storage.
  • Take meter readings (water, electricity) at both old and new homes.
  • Return all keys, remote controls, and parking tags.
  • Update your address with banks, subscriptions, and government records.
  • Make sure utilities are live at the new home before you arrive.
✅ Checklist

Home Helpers & Cleaning

Housekeeping routines, hygiene, and working with domestic staff

🧹
Daily & Weekly Cleaning Routine
Structure keeps a home consistently clean:
  • Daily: Wipe kitchen surfaces, wash dishes, sweep high-traffic floors.
  • Weekly: Mop floors, vacuum sofas and rugs, clean bathrooms, wipe mirrors.
  • Monthly: Descale taps, clean behind appliances, wipe baseboards.
  • Keep a small cleaning caddy per floor — it eliminates trips to fetch supplies.
🗓️ Routine
🧴
Natural Cleaning Alternatives
Effective and budget-friendly homemade cleaners:
  • All-purpose spray: Water + white vinegar + few drops of dish soap.
  • Limescale remover: Half lemon rubbed on taps, left for 10 minutes.
  • Oven degreaser: Baking soda paste + vinegar spray.
  • Fabric freshener: Diluted fabric softener in a spray bottle.
🌿 Natural
👩‍👧
Working with Domestic Staff
Set yourself and your helper up for success:
  • Create a written task list with priorities — don't assume shared expectations.
  • Provide necessary cleaning products and tools on day one.
  • Schedule a weekly check-in to give feedback and discuss any issues.
  • Store important documents and valuables securely — this protects both parties.
💼 Management

Furniture Stores & Purchasing

Buying wisely, arranging rooms, and caring for new pieces

🛋️
Arranging Rooms for Better Flow
Good layout makes a room feel larger and more comfortable:
  • Don't push all furniture against walls — floating arrangements feel more welcoming.
  • Create a focal point (TV, fireplace, window) and arrange seating facing it.
  • Use an area rug to define a seating zone in an open-plan room.
  • Keep natural light paths clear — don't block windows with tall furniture.
🏠 Design
🧼
Caring for Different Upholstery
Different fabrics, different rules:
  • Fabric sofas: Vacuum weekly. Spot-clean with mild soap and cold water.
  • Leather: Wipe with a damp cloth only — never use bleach. Condition twice a year.
  • Velvet: Brush in one direction with a soft brush. Never rub.
  • Rotate cushions regularly to distribute wear evenly.
✨ Care
🌞
Sun & Humidity Protection
The two biggest enemies of furniture in the region:
  • Use UV-blocking window film or curtains to prevent fading and fabric degradation.
  • Keep wood furniture away from air conditioner vents — sudden temperature changes cause cracking.
  • Use a dehumidifier in damp rooms — excess moisture warps wood and causes fabric mold.
  • Lift — don't drag — furniture when moving it, to protect both the piece and your flooring.
🛡️ Protection

📅 Seasonal Home Maintenance Checklist

Stay ahead of problems with these quarterly checks — organised by season.

🌸 Spring

  • Inspect roof and gutters for winter damage
  • Test all smoke and CO detectors
  • Check window and door seals
  • Service AC before summer heat
  • Deep-clean carpets and upholstery
  • Inspect iron gates for rust

☀️ Summer

  • Check plumbing for leaks (heat can expand pipes)
  • Clean AC filters monthly
  • Inspect wood furniture for heat cracks
  • Check electrical panel for heat-related issues
  • Lubricate all door hinges
  • Treat outdoor metal with anti-rust paint

🍂 Autumn

  • Flush water heater to remove sediment
  • Inspect heating system before winter
  • Check all exterior wood for paint peeling
  • Clean oven and kitchen exhaust fan
  • Inspect all window grilles and locks
  • Deep-clean behind large appliances

❄️ Winter

  • Insulate exposed pipes to prevent freezing
  • Check doors and windows for cold drafts
  • Test ground fault circuit interrupters
  • Check for condensation buildup on windows
  • Inspect mattresses — flip or rotate them
  • Declutter and organise storage areas
